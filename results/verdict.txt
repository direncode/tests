Reasoning:
  a) The framework operates in continuous latent spaces with
     known energy functions - not applicable to discrete
     symbolic reasoning or language understanding.

  b) Hierarchical modulation provides local exploration benefits
     but does not address:
     - Compositional generalization
     - Causal reasoning
     - Long-term memory and planning
     - Symbol grounding

  c) The manifold geometry assumption (data lies on M) is
     appropriate for perception but unclear for cognition.

HOWEVER, two relevant observations:
  1) Periodic modulation resembles neural oscillations in
     biological systems - could inform architectural choices
  2) Riemannian structure preserves geometric invariants -
     potentially useful for equivariant representations

These are speculative connections, not evidence.
